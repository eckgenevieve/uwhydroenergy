About Our Team


We are a UW-Madison student organization dedicated to promoting hydropower research and development. Our commitment to clean energy is exhibited through community connection initiatives, Department of Energy-sponsored competition participation, and direct engagement with industry partners. We are sponsored by the Hydropower Foundation.

Goals

Opportunities


  • Experiential Development

    Organizing participation in DOE competitions, engineering workshops, and industry events.
  • Community Involvement

    Participating in campus and community events, participating in community clean-ups, and volunteering to promote sustainable energy initiatives.
  • Career Exposure

    Attending local industry conferences, spreading awareness on campus and online career opportunities, and offering networking events, interviews, and Q&As.

  • Volunteering and Community Involvement

    Hosting tables and panels to local communities and schools to promote hydropower education.

  • Seminars and Panels

    Hosting seminars, interviews, and panels via our network of industry professionals to the local UW-Madison community. Topics range from hydropower 101 to specific research categories.

  • Networking Events

    Organizing events to connect students with industry professionals.

  • Hands-On Experience

    Hydropower Collegiate Competition participation gives members opportunities to engage in industry-standard research, siting, design, testing, and reporting methods for hydropower system development.

  • And More!

    We are committed to engaging in hydropower learning opportunities outside of the classroom. Pilot year events included site visits, such as touring the Chalk Hill Dam outside of Green Bay, and attending the annual Midwest Regional Hydropower Conference.

    We are eager to continue providing our members with diverse experiences and learning opportunities.

2027 Hydropower Collegiate Competition


The HCC provides hands-on and educational opportunities for teams across the US to develop hydropower for non-powered dams, in-conduit systems. and pumped storage through various challenges listed below. This year, 15 collegiate teams are competing, including our UW-Madison Hydro Energy Team. Teams will attend the Final Event in late April, and winners can earn up to $15,000 in prize money.

Our team will publish our competition deliverables and updates below throughout the school year.

Siting

Challenge consists of assessing and selecting a site for retrofit, PSH system, or in-conduit system.
